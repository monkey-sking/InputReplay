# Licensing and Commercial Distribution

InputReplay uses an **open-core-friendly** licensing structure built around the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0).

## Open-source core

Files covered by MPL-2.0 remain under MPL-2.0 when modified and distributed. If you distribute modifications to covered source files, those modifications must remain available under MPL-2.0.

MPL is file-level copyleft. A larger work can combine MPL-covered files with separately licensed files, provided the MPL obligations for covered files are preserved.

## Why this model

InputReplay is intended to grow through open-source adoption first. The license should:

- let users inspect a system-level input utility they are trusting with Accessibility permission;
- allow community contributions and IME compatibility fixes;
- prevent closed forks from silently taking modified core files private;
- still allow separately licensed store integrations, services, enterprise features, and premium modules later.

## Possible future paid offerings

The maintainers may later offer paid products or services such as:

- signed/notarized official distribution;
- Mac App Store / Setapp editions;
- premium onboarding, diagnostics, UI, or policy features;
- cross-device settings sync;
- team / enterprise deployment and policy management;
- optional premium integrations or services implemented in separate modules;
- commercial support.

The existence of paid offerings does not revoke the MPL-2.0 rights already granted for the open-source core.

## Brand is separate from code

MPL-2.0 does not grant rights to the InputReplay name, logo, icon, or official distribution identity. See `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## Contributor note

For now, contributions are accepted under the repository's MPL-2.0 license without a separate CLA. If the project later needs dual licensing that requires broader relicensing rights, that policy must be introduced transparently before accepting contributions under new terms; existing contributors' rights cannot simply be assumed.

## Third-party code

Third-party dependencies remain subject to their own licenses. Do not copy source from FlicKey or other reference projects into InputReplay unless the exact license compatibility and attribution requirements have been reviewed first. Architectural research is fine; source-code reuse is a separate licensing decision.

This document explains project intent and is not legal advice.
